The quiet craft of software

Good software is rarely loud. It does its work without demanding attention, and the people who use it seldom notice the care that went into it.

A translation tool, for instance, should stay out of the way. It reads the page, offers its help quietly, and never breaks the reading you came for. This paragraph contains a link and some bold emphasis that should survive translation intact.

  • Isolation: never pollute the host page.
  • Quality: context-aware, faithful translation.
  • Restraint: a small, elegant core.
